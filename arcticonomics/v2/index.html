When the world is at peace,
the Arctic is worthless.

When the world breaks,
the Arctic is priceless.

The Arctic economy is counter-cyclical: it moves in the opposite direction to the world’s.

V · Greenland

Four billion years old.
Untouched. Suddenly wanted.

Greenland’s crust has barely changed since the Earth was young, which keeps its minerals pure. About $1.8 trillion of known resources at October 2025 prices, by a rough FRONTTIER estimate, under an island that is 80% ice. Greenland has not changed. The world around it has.

VI · The great Nordic paradox

The Nordics move forward
when the world moves backward.

Democratic in an autocratic world. Stable in a chaotic one. United in a fragmented one. Their story says peace; their economy says exception. Time to rethink the idea of the Nordics.
